# Quality Control and Validation

## Purpose

Quality control ensures that the chemistry dataset is accurate, consistent, and suitable for AI training.

## Validation Criteria

Each record should be checked for:

- Scientific accuracy
- Clear question wording
- Correct answer
- Correct chemistry topic
- Appropriate difficulty level
- Consistent formatting

## Common Errors to Check

### 1. Incorrect Scientific Information
Answers must agree with established chemistry principles.

### 2. Wrong Topic Classification
The question should be assigned to the chemistry topic that best represents its main concept.

### 3. Incorrect Difficulty
Difficulty should reflect the level of chemistry knowledge required to understand or solve the question.

### 4. Incomplete Answers
Answers should provide enough explanation to address the question accurately.

### 5. Formatting Errors
Dataset fields should follow the same structure throughout the file.

## Quality Review Process

1. Review the question.
2. Verify the scientific answer.
3. Check the topic label.
4. Check the difficulty label.
5. Correct any errors.
6. Perform a final consistency check.

## Quality Goal

The goal is to produce high-quality, reliable training data that can be used in AI-powered chemistry education and question-answering systems.
