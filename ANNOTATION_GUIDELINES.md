# Chemistry Question Annotation Guidelines

## Purpose

These guidelines explain how chemistry questions and answers should be reviewed and labeled for AI training.

## Annotation Fields

### Question
The chemistry question being presented to the AI system.

### Answer
A scientifically accurate and understandable response to the question.

### Topic
The main chemistry area covered by the question.

Examples:
- Organic Chemistry
- Physical Chemistry
- Analytical Chemistry
- Inorganic Chemistry

### Difficulty
The estimated level required to understand or solve the question.

- Beginner
- Intermediate
- Advanced

## Quality Standards

Each question and answer should be:

1. Scientifically accurate.
2. Clear and understandable.
3. Relevant to the selected topic.
4. Free from unnecessary information.
5. Appropriately classified by difficulty.

## Review Process

Each annotation should be checked for:

- Accuracy
- Completeness
- Relevance
- Appropriate difficulty
- Consistent labeling

## AI Training Goal

The purpose of this annotation process is to produce reliable, structured chemistry data that can support AI training and educational question-answering systems.
